SyncHealth Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026. Applies to the SyncHealth Android app (com.synchealth.app).

What we collect

With your consent via Huawei ID sign-in, SyncHealth reads your steps, calories, heart rate and sleep data from Huawei Health Kit. It also stores your OAuth tokens and app settings on your device.

How we use it

The data is used solely to write it to Android Health Connect on your own device, so that apps you choose (such as Google Fit or MyFitnessPal) can display it. It is not used for advertising, analytics, profiling or any other purpose.

Sharing and storage

SyncHealth has no servers. Your data is not uploaded to the developer or any third party. Once in Health Connect, access is controlled by you in Android settings.

Your choices

You can sign out of Huawei, revoke the app's Health Kit authorization in your Huawei account, revoke Health Connect permissions, delete the data written by the app (in-app button or Health Connect settings), or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes all locally stored tokens and settings.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.
